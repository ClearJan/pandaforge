# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The project community is expected to make participation a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of
expertise.

## Behavior

Be constructive in code review, critique ideas rather than people, accept
critique of your own work the same way, and keep discussion on the
engineering. The project's review culture is evidence-based — "show me the
measurement" is a question about the code, never about the author.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or PRs that violate this standard
and may ban repeat offenders. Reports go to the repository owner.
